## 📊 一级标签：公司/项目

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  tag AS "公司/项目",
  length(rows) AS "笔记数量",
  choice(length(rows) > 50, "✅ 充分", choice(length(rows) > 10, "⚠️ 一般", "❌ 不足")) AS "状态"
FROM ""
WHERE contains(tags, "#ByteDance") OR contains(tags, "#NIO") OR contains(tags, "#蔚来") OR contains(tags, "#Xiaomi") OR contains(tags, "#Meituan") OR contains(tags, "#DingDong") OR contains(tags, "#叮咚买菜") OR contains(tags, "#Siemens") OR contains(tags, "#YanFeng") OR contains(tags, "#Self") OR contains(tags, "#SJTU") OR contains(tags, "#SHU")
FLATTEN choice(contains(tags, "#ByteDance"), "#ByteDance", choice(contains(tags, "#NIO"), "#NIO", choice(contains(tags, "#蔚来"), "#蔚来", choice(contains(tags, "#Xiaomi"), "#Xiaomi", choice(contains(tags, "#Meituan"), "#Meituan", choice(contains(tags, "#DingDong"), "#DingDong", choice(contains(tags, "#叮咚买菜"), "#叮咚买菜", choice(contains(tags, "#Siemens"), "#Siemens", choice(contains(tags, "#YanFeng"), "#YanFeng", choice(contains(tags, "#Self"), "#Self", choice(contains(tags, "#SJTU"), "#SJTU", choice(contains(tags, "#SHU"), "#SHU", "其他")))))))))))) AS tag
GROUP BY tag
SORT length(rows) DESC
```

## 🚨 缺失标签的笔记

### 无公司/项目标签

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  file.link AS "笔记",
  file.folder AS "文件夹",
  choice(contains(file.folder, "ByteDance"), "#ByteDance", choice(contains(file.folder, "NIO"), "#NIO", choice(contains(file.folder, "Xiaomi"), "#Xiaomi", choice(contains(file.folder, "Meituan"), "#Meituan", choice(contains(file.folder, "DingDong"), "#DingDong", choice(contains(file.folder, "Siemens"), "#Siemens", choice(contains(file.folder, "YanFeng"), "#YanFeng", choice(contains(file.folder, "SJTU"), "#SJTU", choice(contains(file.folder, "SHU"), "#SHU", choice(contains(file.folder, "Self"), "#Self", "未知")))))))))) AS "建议添加"

WHERE !contains(tags, "#ByteDance") AND !contains(tags, "#NIO") AND !contains(tags, "#蔚来") AND !contains(tags, "#Xiaomi") AND !contains(tags, "#Meituan") AND !contains(tags, "#DingDong") AND !contains(tags, "#叮咚买菜") AND !contains(tags, "#Siemens") AND !contains(tags, "#YanFeng") AND !contains(tags, "#Self") AND !contains(tags, "#SJTU") AND !contains(tags, "#SHU") AND !contains(file.folder, ".obsidian") AND !contains(file.folder, ".git") AND !contains(file.folder, ".claude") AND !contains(file.folder, ".agents") AND !contains(file.folder, ".opencode") AND !contains(file.folder, "node_modules") AND !contains(file.folder, "assets") AND !contains(file.folder, "Clippings")
```

## 📈 标签使用趋势

### 最近 7 天更新的笔记标签分布

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  tag AS "标签",
  length(rows) AS "更新数量"
FROM ""
WHERE file.ctime >= date(today) - dur(7 days) OR file.mtime >= date(today) - dur(7 days)
FLATTEN tags AS tag
GROUP BY tag
SORT length(rows) DESC
LIMIT 20
```

## 📊 综合统计

### 按文件夹统计标签覆盖

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  file.folder AS "文件夹",
  length(rows) AS "笔记数量",
  choice(length(filter(rows, r => length(r.tags) > 0)) / length(rows) > 0.8, "✅ 良好", choice(length(filter(rows, r => length(r.tags) > 0)) / length(rows) > 0.5, "⚠️ 一般", "❌ 不足")) AS "标签覆盖"
FROM ""
WHERE !contains(file.folder, ".obsidian") AND !contains(file.folder, ".git") AND !contains(file.folder, ".claude") AND !contains(file.folder, ".agents") AND !contains(file.folder, ".opencode") AND !contains(file.folder, "node_modules") AND !contains(file.folder, "assets")
GROUP BY file.folder

LIMIT 20
```
